## Brand Identity & Heraldic Elements

The official **MMP Manpower Services** logo represents strength, trust, prestige, and national workforce empowerment across Bharat:

1. **Royal 5-Peak Crown with Precious Jewels**:
   - **Tiranga-Inspired Gemstones**: Saffron topazes, white diamonds, and emerald green cabochons embedded along the royal arch.
   - **5 Golden Pearl Finials**: Radiating excellence across candidate screening, verification, training, placement, and SLA compliance.

2. **Crystalline Tiranga Soaring Wings**:
   - **Left Wing (Saffron / Orange)**: Dynamic crystalline feathers symbolizing the passion, energy, and relentless drive of India's youth and workforce.
   - **Right Wing (Emerald Green)**: Brilliant crystalline emerald feathers representing economic prosperity, stability, organizational growth, and corporate trust.

3. **Stylized 3D "MMP" Monogram**:
   - Inverted triangular shield with precision gold bevels set against a deep emerald green guilloché backdrop.
   - 6 vertical fluted 3D golden columns tapering into a central spear point, forming the distinctive monogram **M · M · P**.

4. **Golden Handshake of Trust**:
   - Clasping golden hands at the heart of the emblem with sculpted winged cuffs.
   - Represents the core mission: bridging job seekers and employers, candidate aspirations and business growth.

5. **Integrated Official Typography**:
   - **MANPOWER**: Rendered in majestic 3D Roman serif capitals — **MAN** in radiant crystalline Saffron/Gold and **POWER** in vibrant crystalline Emerald Green/Gold.
   - **— SERVICES —**: Clean, widely spaced golden geometric capitals flanked by horizontal ornamental spearhead rules.
   - **Official Tagline**:
     **"YOUR WORKFORCE. OUR STRENGTH. YOUR SUCCESS."**
     Flanked by a central emerald diamond emblem and subtle tricolor underline rule.
