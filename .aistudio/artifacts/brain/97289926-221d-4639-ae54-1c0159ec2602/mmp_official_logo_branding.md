## Brand Identity & Heraldic Elements

The official **MMP Manpower Services** logo represents strength, trust, prestige, and connection across Bharat's workforce and corporate enterprise sectors:

1. **Royal 5-Peak Crown with Precious Jewels**:
   - **Central Teardrop Ruby**: Symbolizes passion, dedication, and the energy of India's working youth.
   - **Twin Blue Sapphire Cabochons**: Flanking jewels representing integrity, transparency, and corporate trust.
   - **5 Golden Pearl Finials**: Radiating excellence across candidate screening, verification, training, placement, and SLA compliance.

2. **Stylized 3D "MMP" Monogram**:
   - Inverted triangular shield with precision gold bevels.
   - 6 vertical fluted 3D golden columns tapering into a central spear point, forming the distinctive letters **M · M · P**.

3. **Golden Handshake of Trust**:
   - Clasping golden hands at the heart of the emblem.
   - Represents the core mission: bridging job seekers and employers, candidate aspirations and business growth.
   - Detailed cuffs with ornate leaf engravings and firm interlocking grip.

4. **Magnificent Sweeping Eagle Wings**:
   - Multi-tiered feathered plumage soaring outwards and upwards toward the royal crown.
   - Symbolizes career elevation, mobility across Karnataka and PAN-India, and unlimited organizational growth.

5. **Integrated Official Typography**:
   - **MANPOWER**: Rendered in majestic, high-contrast 3D golden Roman serif capitals.
   - **— SERVICES —**: Clean, widely spaced golden geometric capitals flanked by horizontal gold rules.
